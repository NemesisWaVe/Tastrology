3. **Five qualifying referrals** (section 5).

## 5. What counts as a qualifying referral

A referral qualifies only when **all** of these are true:
- the person opened **your** invite link (a card link or home link with your invite code). If they opened several people's links, the **first** one in the last 30 days counts;
- they then created a **brand-new** Tastrology account with a verified email;
- that account completed **one fresh reading** (a new card, not a demo or a repeat);
- they are a real, separate person, 18+ and eligible under section 3, and the referral passes our checks (section 7).

Link visits, likes, follows or shares on their own don't count. You'll see a meter such as "3 of 5 friends read their stars". It shows numbers only, never your friends' names or emails.

## 6. Sharing fairly

- Share your link any way you like. **We will never ask you to upload contacts or give us friends' phone numbers or emails, and we never message anyone on your behalf.**
- **Say you get a reward.** The default share text includes a line like *"I get a free print if you join through my link"*. Please keep it, or say something equally clear, especially on public posts. If you are a creator posting to an audience, put the disclosure at the start (for example "#referral" or "#ad").
- **No spam:** no bulk messages to strangers, comment-spam, group-blasting people who haven't agreed, or bought sign-ups.
- **Don't mislead:** don't promise money, say anything is "guaranteed" beyond these rules, or suggest a brand or artist endorses Tastrology.

## 7. Checks, fraud and disqualification

- We check referrals for self-referral, repeated devices or networks, duplicate or disposable emails, bursts of sign-ups and bots. We store only **salted hashes** of IP addresses and device ids for this, for 30 days.
- **Shared networks are common** (family Wi-Fi, college or office networks, mobile carriers). A shared network alone **flags** a referral for review. It doesn't reject it automatically.
- **A person reviews every flagged referral and every winner** before anything ships. If we reject a referral or an entry, we tell you why, and you can ask us to look again.
- We may disqualify entries, cancel referrals or cancel unshipped rewards that break these rules or the Terms. The slot then passes to the next eligible person.

> **Internal: why the rules look like this (remove before publishing)**
> 1. **No lottery or gambling.** The reward is guaranteed to anyone who meets objective conditions, in order of arrival, with no chance element and no entry fee. That keeps it outside the Public Gambling Act 1867 and state gaming and lottery laws, and outside the Prize Competitions Act 1955. Courts read that Act down to gambling-type competitions, and it applies only in some states. **Never** switch to "random winners among sharers". That adds chance, and in some US states referral-only entry also counts as consideration.
> 2. **The referee must never need to pay.** If a friend had to buy a credit to "count", the promotion would require a purchase. `FREE_MINTS` must stay ≥ 1 for as long as the promotion runs.
> 3. **"Free gift" must be really given.** Under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, offering gifts or prizes without intending to provide them as offered is an unfair trade practice. The design doc's plan to "collect claims now, print once funded" is only lawful with a **committed ship-by date** and a fallback, both stated up front (section 8). Don't launch the claim button without them.
> 4. **No "50% off" claims.** The design doc says "free print plus about 50% off shipping". A discount claim needs a genuine reference price; otherwise it is a misleading ad (CCPA India Misleading Advertisements Guidelines 2022). Hiding a shipping fee until the last step is "drip pricing" under the Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023. Say "Free print. Shipping ₹__", or make shipping free.
>
> **Update 2026-10-17:** the founder clarified the offer is **50% off the print price; shipping is always paid in full**. That is fine if (a) the full print price is a genuine price the print is actually sold at, (b) the offer shows print price, discount and shipping together up front, e.g. "Print ₹249 (₹499) + shipping ₹__", and (c) shipping carries no hidden margin.
> 5. **Scarcity must be real.** "Only N left" must come from the real counter. False urgency is a listed dark pattern.
> 6. **Disclosure.** ASCI's influencer rules, the CCPA India endorsement guidelines (2023) and the FTC Endorsement Guides all treat a reward for recommending as a material connection. Pre-filling the disclosure in the share text is the cheapest fix.
> 7. **Friends' data.** Contact uploads or "invite by phone number" would make us process data about people who never agreed (DPDP, GDPR) and invite spam complaints (TRAI rules for SMS). The design already avoids this; keep it that way.
