# Growth Schemes: Lawful, Near-Zero-Cost Ways to Win Users

> **Draft, 2026-10-17. Not yet reviewed: a qualified lawyer must review this before launch. It is not legal advice.**
> Written for a founder with $0 who wants aggressive, attention-grabbing growth. "By hook or crook" here means **bold but lawful and honest**. Indian law is the main lens, with US basics noted. Read with `promo-first-100.md` and `open-questions.md`.

> **The short version**
> - **Run these three at launch:** (1) **Genesis serials:** the first 1,000 accounts get a numbered "Genesis" mark on their digital cards; (2) **two-sided referral unlocks:** you and your friend each get a free extra reading, and 5 friends unlock a holo frame; (3) **"Card of the Week"**, a free-entry creativity contest judged on stated criteria, with a digital prize and one print.
> - **Keep the first-100 free print** only if you can fund ~₹10–25k and commit to a ship-by date. Otherwise turn it into a **print waitlist with referral jumps**, which promises nothing physical.
> - **Never:** random paid packs, spin-the-wheel, cash for referrals, fake scarcity, contact uploads, auto-posting, celebrity or brand names in ads, lucky draws among sharers.

---

## The rules every scheme must pass

1. **No chance plus consideration.** If a winner is picked by luck, entry must be completely free, with no purchase, payment or required sign-up of others. In India, prefer **no chance at all**: first-come or skill-judged. Lucky draws used to promote a business are risky under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 (unfair trade practices) and state lottery laws.
2. **Nothing random for money.** Never sell random outcomes or let credits convert to cash. That is loot-box territory and, with stakes, an "online money game" under the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act 2025.
3. **Honest scarcity and pricing.** Counters are real, "limited" means limited, and "free" means free. Fees are shown up front. False urgency, drip pricing, bait-and-switch and confirm-shaming are listed dark patterns (CCPA India Guidelines 2023).
4. **Disclose rewards for recommending.** Required by ASCI, CCPA India's endorsement guidelines and the FTC Endorsement Guides. Pre-fill the disclosure in the share text.
5. **No friends' data.** Don't ask for contacts, phone numbers or emails, and don't message anyone on a user's behalf (DPDP Act, TRAI anti-spam rules, US CAN-SPAM/TCPA).
6. **Promise only what you'll deliver.** Offering a gift without intending to provide it as offered is an unfair trade practice.
7. **No third-party names in marketing.** "What's Shah Rukh Khan's taste sign?" or "Get your Nike sign" uses personality rights and trademarks to sell. That's a line, not a grey area.

## Schemes assessed

Legend: **Cost** = cash cost to us. **India risk** / **US risk**: Low, Medium or High.

### 1. Genesis serials (founder / early-adopter badges)
- **What:** accounts 1–1,000 get "Genesis #0042" on every digital card they mint during the launch season, plus a profile badge.
- **Cost:** ₹0. It's a field on the profile.
- **India risk:** Low. **US risk:** Low.
- **Keep it lawful:** make the cap real and visible. Don't call it "valuable", "investment" or "appreciating", and don't let people sell serials. Say: "A thank-you mark for our first 1,000 readers. It has no cash value."
- **Verdict: RUN.** It's the best zero-cost status lever. Scarcity is honest because the serial count is fixed.

### 2. Two-sided referral unlocks (digital perks instead of physical goods)
- **What:** when a friend joins through your link and mints, **both** of you get +1 free reading. At 5 friends you unlock a holo foil frame or a Shadow Pack.
- **Cost:** a few extra Qloo and LLM calls, within free tiers at this scale. No shipping.
- **India risk:** Low. **US risk:** Low (disclosure only).
- **Keep it lawful:** guaranteed rewards, no chance. Credits have no cash value and can't be transferred. Friends never need to pay. Built-in "I get a free reading if you join" text. Same fraud checks as the print promotion.
- **Verdict: RUN.** It does what the first-100 print does, at about 1% of the cost, and with no fulfilment risk.

### 3. "Card of the Week": a free-entry skill/creativity contest (UGC)
- **What:** post your card with a one-line caption ("my taste sign explained in 10 words") and #Tastrology, or submit it on-site. A named judge picks the best by published criteria (originality 50%, humour 30%, clarity 20%). Prize: a Genesis-style "Featured" frame plus 10 credits; once funded, one print a week.
- **Cost:** ₹0, plus about ₹100/week if a print is added.
- **India risk:** Low, since it is skill-based, free, and judged on criteria. **US risk:** Low (skill contest; no purchase).
- **Keep it lawful:** short official rules covering entry period, criteria, judge, prize and how the winner is announced. 18+. Entrants grant a non-exclusive licence to repost their entry. **Don't require "tag 3 friends" or "follow us" to enter**: Instagram's promotion rules ban inaccurate tagging, and forced follows look like consideration. Include a line saying the platform isn't involved.
- **Verdict: RUN.** It creates shareable content and needs nobody's contact details.

### 4. First-100 free print for 5 referrals (the founder's idea)
- **What:** see `promo-first-100.md`.
- **Cost:** about ₹10–25k for 100 prints plus postage. Not $0.
- **India risk:** Low **if** first-come, free for referees, with a committed ship date and no "50% off" claim. High if claims are collected with no funding or date (an unfair trade practice).
- **Keep it lawful:** as drafted, including the ship-by date and fallback reward.
- **Verdict: RUN ONLY IF FUNDED.** Otherwise replace it with scheme 5.

### 5. Print waitlist with position jumps for referrals
- **What:** "Prints are coming. Join the waitlist." Each qualified referral moves you up 10 places. When prints launch, people are invited in order (to buy, or the first N get them free if funded then).
- **Cost:** ₹0 now.
- **India risk:** Low. **US risk:** Low.
- **Keep it lawful:** the queue must be real, with positions computed honestly and not shuffled to create urgency. Don't promise a date or a free item you haven't committed to. Say exactly what the waitlist gets you (early access, not a free item, unless you commit).
- **Verdict: GOOD FALLBACK.** Swap it in for scheme 4 if the founder can't fund prints.

### 6. Limited drops (seasonal frames and editions)
- **What:** a "Diwali Edition" or "Hackathon Edition" card frame, available to mint only during a stated window.
- **Cost:** ₹0 (design time).
- **India risk:** Low if the window is real. **US risk:** Low.
- **Keep it lawful:** state the end date up front and actually retire it. No fake "ending in 10:00" timers that reset (false urgency). Free, or included with a normal reading. Never sell drops as random.
- **Verdict: RUN after launch.** It gives people a reason to come back each month.

### 7. Rarity leaderboard
- **What:** an opt-in public board of the rarest cards this season, showing only card codes or handles.
- **Cost:** ₹0 (the histogram already exists).
- **India risk:** Low with opt-in; Medium if names are shown by default (DPDP: publishing personal data needs consent). **US risk:** Low.
- **Keep it lawful:** opt-in only. Show the handle and archetype, not loves or real names. **No prizes for the top spots.** Prizes for rarity would reward gaming the inputs, and prizes for top referrers reward spam.
- **Verdict: RUN (no prizes).**

### 8. Creator and college ambassador program
- **What:** campus or creator ambassadors get a personal code, free credits, a "Founding Ambassador" badge, and a print once funded.
- **Cost:** free credits; later a few prints.
- **India risk:** Medium. Disclosure duties fall on both sides, and ambassadors who post badly reflect on us. **US risk:** Medium (FTC: we must monitor disclosures).
- **Keep it lawful:** a one-page written agreement covering 18+, disclosure on every post (#ad or #partner at the start), no spam, no false claims, and no use of brand or celebrity names. **Flat perks only, no multi-level rewards** (rewards for recruiting recruiters look like a pyramid under the Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act 1978 and the Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules 2021). No cash payouts until the business is set up for TDS and GST.
- **Verdict: RUN SMALL (10–20 people) after launch.**

### 9. Prize draw ("share and win")
- **What:** "Everyone who shares is entered into a draw for 10 prints."
- **Cost:** low.
- **India risk:** **High.** A chance-based draw to promote a business draws scrutiny under the CPA 2019 unfair-trade-practice rules and state lottery laws, and requiring sharing or sign-ups can count as consideration. **US risk:** Medium. It needs a free alternative method of entry, full official rules and odds disclosure, and if prizes total more than $5,000, registration and bonding in New York and Florida.
- **Verdict: DON'T.** Use the skill contest (scheme 3) instead.

### 10. Pre-orders for prints, funded before printing
- **What:** take payment now and print when there are enough orders.
- **Cost:** ₹0, since customers fund it.
- **India risk:** Medium to High **today**. It needs live payments (Razorpay KYC and a registered entity), GST invoicing, a stated delivery date, and automatic refunds if it isn't met. Above all it's a paid product built on Qloo output, which is blocked until Qloo gives written OK. **US risk:** Medium (FTC Mail Order Rule: ship within the stated time or offer refunds).
- **Verdict: LATER.** Only after the entity, Razorpay live and Qloo permission are in place. Then it's a good way to fund prints.

### 11. "Pay shipping only" prints
- **What:** the print is free and the user pays postage.
- **Cost:** print cost (~₹20–40 each) is ours.
- **India risk:** Medium. "Free" is misleading if the "shipping" charge has margin built in. It also needs live payments (same blockers as scheme 10).
- **Keep it lawful:** charge actual postage, show it on the offer itself, and don't call it a discount.
- **Verdict: LATER**, with scheme 10.

### 12. Battle challenges (already built)
- **What:** "My card vs yours". The `/vs` link invites a friend to mint and fight.
- **Cost:** ₹0.
- **India risk:** Low, as long as there are **no stakes or prizes for winning**. Add stakes and it moves toward an online money game. **US risk:** Low.
- **Verdict: RUN.** Lean on it in share copy. It's the most natural viral loop and needs no incentive at all.

## Lines not to cross (blunt list)

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| Paid random packs, spin-the-wheel, mystery boxes | Gambling and loot-box law; the Online Gaming Act 2025 once anything converts to value; a dark pattern |
| Cash, UPI or gift-card payouts for referrals | Money handling, TDS and tax; spam incentive; pyramid optics if layered |
| Fake "only 3 left" or resetting timers | False urgency (Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023), misleading ads |
| "Free" with hidden or padded shipping | Drip pricing; misleading "free" claim |
| Contact upload or "invite from phonebook" | Processing third parties' data without consent (DPDP); spam (TRAI) |
| Auto-posting to users' social accounts, pre-ticked marketing consent | Forced action and consent defects; platform bans |
| Celebrity or brand names in ads or demos ("Virat's taste sign") | Personality rights injunctions (Delhi and Bombay HCs); trademark and false endorsement |
| Buying followers, reviews or sign-ups; fake testimonials | Fake reviews are banned (CCPA India, FTC Consumer Reviews Rule 2024) and against platform rules |
| Promising prints "soon" with no budget or date | Unfair trade practice (gift not provided as offered) |
| Letting users resell cards or serials for money | Qloo resale clause; securities and gambling optics; money transmission |

## Ranking

1. **Two-sided referral unlocks** (scheme 2): biggest growth per rupee, zero fulfilment risk.
2. **Genesis serials** (scheme 1): zero cost, honest scarcity, and it makes the first week special.
3. **Card of the Week skill contest** (scheme 3): content engine, no data liabilities.
4. Battle challenges (scheme 12): already built; just promote it.
5. First-100 free print (scheme 4), **only if funded**; otherwise the print waitlist (scheme 5).
6. Limited drops (scheme 6) and the rarity leaderboard (scheme 7), after launch.
7. Ambassadors (scheme 8), small and with written terms.
8. Pre-orders and pay-shipping prints (schemes 10–11), after Qloo OK, entity and Razorpay live.
9. Prize draw (scheme 9): don't.

**Recommendation for launch day:** run schemes 2, 1 and 3, and promote the existing battles. Run the first-100 print alongside them only if the founder sets aside the print budget and a ship-by date. If not, launch the print waitlist and keep "first 100 prints free" as the reward for the top of the waitlist once funded.
