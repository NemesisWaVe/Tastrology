**A card is:**
- a personalised, entertainment-only image and data record, viewable in your deck and by anyone you give its link to;

**A card is not:**
- money, a voucher, stored value, a security, a financial product or an investment;
- a cryptocurrency, token or NFT. Nothing is recorded on any blockchain;
- a claim on any brand, artist or product shown on it;
- guaranteed to keep any value, rarity tier or resale price. Rarity is a description of how unusual your tastes look in our model. It is not scarcity created to make cards valuable, and we may recalibrate how it is calculated.

**We do not run a resale market, and we do not buy cards back.** You may not use Tastrology to sell, auction or trade cards or accounts for money unless we launch a feature that clearly allows it.
